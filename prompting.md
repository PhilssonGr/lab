## What it is

Prompting is how you state the task for this turn: the instruction, the material, and the constraints. A weak prompt and a strong prompt can ask for the same outcome and still give the model different jobs.

## Why it matters

**Cost.** A vague prompt often leads to another turn to get something you can use.

**Quality.** Naming the reader, the format, and what to leave out gives the reply a finish line.

**Risk.** An open prompt is easier to steer off the task, including by text inside a document you attached.

## Try today

Rewrite one prompt you use so it names the reader, the output shape, and one thing to leave out.

## Common mistake

Adding adjectives (“be detailed and insightful”) instead of a concrete constraint.
