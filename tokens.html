Tokens

Track · Foundations

01 What it is

A token is a piece of text the model reads or writes. A token may be a whole word, part of a word, a number, or punctuation. Models measure the request and the reply in tokens.

0 approximate tokens

Approximate. Words and punctuation are split apart. A word or number longer than four characters is cut into slices of four. Spaces and line breaks are their own chips. An API tokenizer will not match this count.

02 Why it matters

  • CostHosted models bill by token. A longer prompt and a longer reply both add to the bill.
  • QualityTokens spent on leftover instructions are tokens the reply cannot use.
  • RiskAnything you paste is sent as tokens, including keys, customer text, and private notes.

03 How to apply it

Try today

Take a prompt you reuse. Run it through the splitter. Delete one sentence that does not change the task.

Common mistake

Treating a character count or a word count as the token count.
