About

BuildSpark AI

A practical map and cards for builders who already use AI.

What it is

Each card says what a concept is, why it matters for cost, quality, and risk, and one thing to try. The map shows the curriculum and which concepts depend on which. Ask matches your words to that list in the browser. It does not call a model.

Concept cards are free. Track packs are later practice, and they are not open yet.

Who it is for

People who already use AI in their work and want a shared map of the ideas. The cards assume you have a prompt, a repo, or a product in front of you. This is not a first lesson in what a chatbot is.

How cards stay current

A live card shows the date it was last checked and a short note of what changed. Those updates are also listed on the changelog. A claim does not get a source line until it can cite a provider document. Until then the card says sources are pending.

One registry is the list of concepts. It drives the map, Ask, the track pages, the sitemap, and the plain-text index at /llms.txt. Live cards also have a markdown version, such as /tokens.md. A name marked coming soon is not a link, because there is no card yet.
